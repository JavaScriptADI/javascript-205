[ CODING WITH AI ]
13

Five rules for coding with AI

  • 01Plan first. For anything bigger than one line, start in plan mode.
  • 02One change at a time. A small diff is a diff you can actually read.
  • 03Read every diff before you accept it. You're the reviewer, not the audience.
  • 04Run it yourself. "Fixed" is a claim; the page is the proof.
  • 05Can't explain a line? Don't keep it. Ask it to explain, or throw the line out.

Never paste passwords or API keys into it. And your homework stays yours: the AI may explain an error, but you write the code. Homework 9 is the exception, because it's about the tool.
